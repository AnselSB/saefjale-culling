# Saefjale: Culling of Flame
Saefjale: Culling of Flame is a startegy, turn-based RPG that is primarily inspired by franchises such as Fire Emblem, Tactics Ogre, and advance wars. The story of the game takes place in the contitent Rrheme Dhia, home to the warring nations of Fuhrentriton and Nairnshire. Our story follows a group of unsuspecting adventurers from across the continent to take part in a culling game to satisfy the carnal desires of a bored noble by the name of Eferant Aberheart. This narrative is based on the plot of a DND campaign that I DM'd back in 2024, I want to learn more about game design at a lower level and thought this could be a fun experiment for this.

### Escape From the Aberheart Estate
Help our heroes Bayram, Brejik, Rowan, Theomedes and Dylan escape the clutches of Eferant and put a stop to his culling games.

### Tech stack
Saefjale will be written in the C language with the primary external dependencies being Raylib, rlgl, raymath, raudio, raygui, rpng and rres. Part of this reason is to get more familiar with Clang since I have primarily only worked with c++ but also to try out raylib. Based on my research of the library I've heard good things about how it helps make game design be done purely through code which is really intriguing to someone like me who struggles with the editor in engines like godot and unity. Additionally, I don't want to write vulkan and opengl directly since I've tried that before and it totally killed my motivation.
